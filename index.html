FAQ

Common questions

Do you serve Houston and Pearland?

Yes. Jais Pressure Washing works in Houston, Pearland and surrounding areas. Call or text (832) 983-0155.

What is soft washing?

Soft washing is a low-pressure cleaning method for homes and delicate exterior surfaces. A cleaning solution breaks down mold, mildew, algae and dirt without relying on high pressure.

Can you wash my whole property?

Yes. A full property wash can include a house soft wash, driveway cleaning and deck cleaning.

Do you clean driveways and walkways?

Yes. Driveways, sidewalks, flagstone pathways and patios.

Do you stain fences?

Yes. We clean and stain wood fences.

Do you work on flower beds?

Yes. We lay fresh mulch, plant new plants, install rock and landscape borders, weed beds and remove old raised beds.

Do you hang holiday lights?

Yes. We install and remove holiday lights.
